# Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing 500 sales orders across regions, categories, products, salespeople, and payment modes.

## Dashboard Preview
[dashboard_preview.png]

## Key Metrics
- **Total Revenue:** 2.69M
- **Total Profit:** 951.91K
- **Total Orders:** 500
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 2K (1,995 units)
- **Average Order Value:** 5.38K

## Visuals Included
- **Sales by Month:** Clustered column & line combo chart comparing monthly Revenue and Profit
- **Sales by Category:** Donut chart showing revenue share across Electronics, Clothing, Home & Kitchen, and Stationery
- **Sales & Profit by Region:** Regional performance breakdown (South, West, North, East)
- **Top 5 Products by Sales:** Table with Revenue, Profit, and Profit %
- **Sales by Salesperson:** Horizontal bar chart comparing rep performance
- **Sales by Payment Mode:** Donut chart of revenue by payment method
